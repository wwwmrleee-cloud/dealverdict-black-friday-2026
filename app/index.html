How DealVerdict works

Verdicts are earned from the numbers. An all-time low is the lowest price any tracker has seen; a strong deal is a deep cut of 25% or more (or $100+ saved); a good deal is a real cut worth taking; a first-ever cut is the first discount on a product that never goes on sale. A routine sale is a small cut on an inflated list price — none is listed here.
